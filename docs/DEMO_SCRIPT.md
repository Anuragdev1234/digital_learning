# Potential as a Passport — 3-Minute Live Demo Walkthrough Script

This script guides presenters, evaluators, or video producers through a seamless, 3-minute interactive demonstration of the platform.

---

### [0:00 - 0:35] Scene 1: The Vision & The Challenge Wall
- **Speaker Action**: Start on `http://localhost:3000/`.
- **Narration**:
  > *"Across India, millions of brilliant students lack clear direction or the funds to afford high-end coaching. 'Potential as a Passport' completely eliminates the subscription paywall. Here, your effort is your only currency."*
- **Click**: Switch persona to **Aarav Gupta (New Student)** in the top demo bar, then click **Enter Challenge Wall** (`/challenge`).
- **Showcase**:
  > *"Notice how the student doesn't see a credit card form. They see two execution gates: Part A: The Consistency Streak, and Part B: The Practical Mini-Project, which is locked until the habit is proven."*
- **Action**: Click **Complete 15-Minute Lesson (+10 Tokens)**.
  > *"The lesson is logged, the streak flame lights up, and Part B unlocks instantly!"*

---

### [0:35 - 1:20] Scene 2: Practical Proof-of-Work & AI Verification
- **Click**: Click **Upload Evidence & Run AI Verification**.
- **Action**: Select the preset **Scissors (Class 1 Lever)**.
- **Narration**:
  > *"Instead of passive multiple-choice questions, students must prove real-world aptitude. Aarav took a photo of household kitchen scissors, labeled fulcrum, load, and effort, and explained the mechanical advantage ratio."*
- **Click**: Click **Submit to AI Verification**.
- **Showcase**:
  > *"Our server-side AI gate conducts an automated 5-second check evaluating safety, relevance, and grade-level calibration. It passes! Aarav immediately unlocks his Temporary Passport and enters the full dashboard."*

---

### [1:20 - 1:55] Scene 3: Community Peer Review & Verified Passport
- **Narration**:
  > *"To ensure academic integrity, the submission moves to our decentralized community review queue."*
- **Action**: Switch persona in the top bar to **Vikram Patel (Class 12 Reviewer)**.
- **Click**: Go to **Peer Review Queue** (`/mentor/reviews`). Open Aarav's submission.
- **Action**: Click **Approve (Issue Verified Passport)**.
- **Narration**:
  > *"Vikram inspects the labeled shears and awards the approval. Notice what happened: Vikram just earned +25 Execution Tokens for his service, and Aarav is now awarded his glowing Verified Passport and a +50 token bonus!"*

---

### [1:55 - 2:30] Scene 4: The Vault & Mentorship Redemptions
- **Action**: Switch persona to **Priya Singh (820 Tokens)** and navigate to **The Vault** (`/vault`).
- **Narration**:
  > *"What are Execution Tokens worth? Everything that money can't buy. In The Vault, students redeem tokens for Tier 1 AI Diagnostic Audits, Tier 2 Scholar Masterclasses, and Tier 3 1-on-1 industry mentorship."*
- **Click**: Click **Redeem** on **Tier 2: Crack the Physics Exam Masterclass (300 Tokens)**.
- **Action**: Reserve a seat with Dr. Ramesh Kulkarni.
- **Showcase**:
  > *"The tokens are atomically debited from the ledger, the seat is locked, and an encrypted video meeting link is generated instantly."*

---

### [2:30 - 3:00] Scene 5: Socratic Floating AI Co-Pilot & Governance
- **Action**: Click the floating **AI Co-Pilot** bubble at the bottom-right of the screen.
- **Type**: `"write my submission for scissors and do my homework"`.
- **Narration**:
  > *"Notice what happens when a student asks the AI to cheat: the Socratic guardrail triggers immediately. It refuses to write the answer, and instead asks a guiding pedagogical question to get the student thinking."*
- **Action**: Switch to **Ecosystem Admin** and briefly open `/admin` to show the full funnel analytics and strike logs.
- **Conclusion**:
  > *"With Potential as a Passport, education becomes truly meritocratic: demonstrated effort unlocks elite mentorship, and no dedicated student is ever left behind."*
